# Northstar Equipment refund policy

Customers can return an unused item within 30 calendar days of delivery. A receipt is required. Shipping fees are not refundable unless the delivered item is defective.

For a defective item reported within 90 calendar days of delivery, Northstar pays return shipping and offers a replacement or refund. The customer chooses between the replacement and refund.

Clearance items cannot be returned for a change of mind. The defective-item policy still applies to clearance items. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within five business days after inspection.
