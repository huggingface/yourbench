# Northstar Equipment support handbook

Support requests arrive through the customer portal. The team responds within two business days. A safety issue is escalated to the duty manager immediately and does not wait for the standard response window.

For a refund request, the agent collects the order number, receipt and delivery date. For a defective item, the agent also requests a description of the defect. Photographs are optional and cannot be required as a condition of a refund.

A manager may extend the unused-item return window from 30 to 45 calendar days when a delivery delay is documented. Agents cannot approve that extension themselves. The extension does not change the defective-item reporting window.
